THE MANIFESTO

What we believe.

Every movement starts with a few things its people refuse to accept. Here are ours.

Sign the manifesto Be one of the first to sign.
  1. 01

    Proof beats paper.

    A certificate says you attended. A shipped project says you can. We only count the second.

  2. 02

    Students shouldn't wait for permission.

    The best AI education on your campus doesn't need a new syllabus. It needs one student to start a chapter.

  3. 03

    AI is a tool you build with, not a topic you study.

    You learn it the way you learn to swim: by getting in.

  4. 04

    The future campus has no back benches.

    Everyone builds. Everyone ships. Everyone teaches what they figured out.

  5. 05

    Builders find builders.

    The right room changes your trajectory faster than any course. We build those rooms.

  6. 06

    India's AI generation will be built on campuses, not just in cities.

    Tier-1, tier-2, tier-3: talent is everywhere. Access shouldn't depend on your college's name.

HOW IT WORKS

From curious to capable in four moves.

Curriculum, projects, mentors and the HOW Score, built around one idea: you learn AI fastest by shipping with it.

  1. STEP 01

    Join

    Get into the community and your first build night. Meet people who are already building, not just talking about it.

  2. STEP 02

    Build

    Pick a real problem and ship a project with AI. Mentors and peers help when you get stuck.

  3. STEP 03

    Prove

    Every build adds to your HOW Score: a public, measurable record of what you can actually do.

  4. STEP 04 · THE BIG ONE

    Lead

    Start a chapter on your campus and bring the movement home. The best way to master something is to teach it.
